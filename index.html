About

This website is meant to display some of my best drawings. It's essentially an informal portfolio. All images, including the favicon, were drawn by me.
My hobby, first and foremost, is still programming. I just like to dabble in art a little. So this is a way for me to show off my art while practicing using HTML and JavaScript. For example, this was the first time I created a form, buttons with images, a vertical sidebar, among other things.
